Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

ScanFree was built so that your documents stay yours. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your data when you use ScanFree.

Your documents are processed privately in your browser

All scanning, cropping, enhancement, and PDF creation happens locally on your device, inside your web browser. Your photos, scans, and PDFs are never uploaded to any server. We have no backend that receives your documents, so we cannot see, read, store, or share them.

Camera use

ScanFree uses your device camera only after you tap "Scan Document" and only while the scanning screen is open. The camera stream is shown on your screen and captured directly into your browser's memory. It is never transmitted anywhere. When you leave the scanning screen, the camera is switched off immediately.

Temporary session data

Scanned pages live only in this browser tab's memory and are automatically and permanently erased when you close the tab or browser, or tap "Start Over". Once the PDF is created, it exists only in your browser until you download, share, or print it.

What we do NOT do

  • No account, registration, email, or phone number.
  • No payment or subscription - the app is completely free.
  • No permanent storage of your documents on any server.
  • No advertising inside the app or inside your PDFs.
  • No watermarks on your documents.
  • No analytics on your documents or images.
  • No use of your documents for AI training or any other purpose.

Local settings

The only thing saved between visits is your light/dark theme preference, stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.

Offline use

ScanFree is a Progressive Web App. After you open it once, your browser keeps a copy of the application itself (not your documents) so the app loads quickly and works offline. This cache contains only app code and icons.

Third parties

ScanFree does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Your internet connection is only used to load the app itself.

Children

ScanFree collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new "last updated" date.
