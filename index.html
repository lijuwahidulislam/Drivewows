How it works

  1. Capture - photograph a document with your camera, or upload a photo.
  2. Adjust - ScanFree finds the page edges; drag the corners to fine-tune.
  3. Enhance - pick a filter and tune brightness, contrast, and sharpness.
  4. Pages - add more scans, reorder, rotate, or delete pages.
  5. Export - create a clean PDF and download, share, or print it.

Frequently asked questions

Is ScanFree really free?

Yes - completely. No account, no email, no payment, no subscription, no premium plan, and no watermark on your PDFs.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Scanning, cropping, enhancement, and PDF creation all happen locally in your browser. Your documents never leave your device.

Which devices are supported?

Any modern phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop with Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, or an Android browser. Camera scanning needs HTTPS, which this site always uses.

Can I scan multiple pages into one PDF?

Yes - scan or upload as many pages as you like, then reorder, rotate, edit, or delete them before creating one combined PDF.

Does the PDF contain ads or watermarks?

Never. Your PDF contains only your pages.

Does it work offline?

After the first load, yes. Install ScanFree from your browser menu and it works offline too.

Private by design

ScanFree has no login, no database, and no document uploads. Temporary scans live only in this browser tab and are erased when your session ends. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
