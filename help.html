Help and How It Works

Quick start

  1. Open ScanFree and tap Scan Document. Allow camera access when your browser asks.
  2. Place your document on a flat surface with good light, and fill the frame. A green outline appears when the page is detected.
  3. Tap the round capture button. (No camera? Use Upload Image and pick photos from your gallery.)
  4. Drag the four circles onto the corners of your document, then tap Confirm Crop. ScanFree straightens and crops the page automatically.
  5. Pick a filter (Auto Enhance, Document, Black and White, Grayscale, Color, or Original), adjust brightness and contrast, rotate if needed, then tap Continue.
  6. Add more pages the same way. Reorder pages by dragging thumbnails, or use the buttons under each page to move, rotate, edit, or delete it.
  7. Tap Preview and Export, give your document a name, choose A4 / Letter / Automatic page size and Standard or High quality, then tap Create PDF.
  8. Download, Share, or Print your PDF - completely free, with no watermark.

Scanning tips for best results

  • Use even, bright light and avoid shadows and glare.
  • Lay the document completely flat and fill most of the frame.
  • Hold the phone steady; tap capture when the green outline hugs the page.
  • For receipts or dark paper, use the Document or Black and White filter.
  • For photos and certificates, Color or Original keeps things vivid.

Page size and quality

A4 and Letter force standard paper sizes. Automatic picks the size and orientation that best matches each page. High quality gives sharper text for printing; Standard gives smaller files that are easy to share on WhatsApp or email.

Install ScanFree as an app

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome / Edge): menu (three dots), then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • Desktop (Chrome / Edge): click the install icon in the address bar.

The installed app works offline and still needs no account.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Camera permission was deniedTap the camera or lock icon in your browser's address bar, set Camera to "Allow", and tap Scan Document again. Or use Upload Image instead.
No camera foundClose other apps or tabs using the camera, then retry. On a desktop without a camera, use Upload Image.
No document detectedImprove the lighting, fill more of the frame, and try "Auto detect" again - or simply drag the four corners onto the page manually.
Text looks fadedUse the Auto Enhance or Document filter, then raise Contrast slightly.
Browser feels slow or the page freezesUse Standard quality, scan fewer pages per document, and close other tabs.
Your device seems low on memoryCreate the PDF in Standard quality, or split a big document into two PDFs.
Share button says sharing is not supportedSome browsers cannot share files. Download the PDF, then share it from your Files or Gallery app.
Refreshed and my pages are gonePages live only in the current session. Just rescan - or download your PDF first next time.
PDF will not openDownload the file again and open it with any PDF viewer (Adobe Acrobat, Chrome, your phone's viewer).

Supported browsers

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Safari (iPhone, iPad, and Mac), and modern Android browsers. Camera scanning needs a secure HTTPS connection, which ScanFree always uses.

Privacy

Your documents are processed privately in your browser - never uploaded, never stored. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
